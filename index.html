About Atmos

Atmos is a frontend-only weather dashboard. Every request — forecast, geocoding, air quality, map tiles — is made directly from your browser to free public APIs. There is no server, no account, and no analytics; your saved cities and settings live only in this browser's local storage.

Weather data: Open‑Meteo. Radar tiles: RainViewer. Map: Leaflet + OpenStreetMap.

  • v2.0 — Dynamic weather/time backgrounds, particles, 3D card tilt, holographic UI, modal system, AI assistant with voice, live radar map, city comparison, air quality detail, weather alerts, animated timeline, settings panel, PWA support.
  • v1.0 — Initial dashboard: search, favourites, hourly & 7-day forecast, geolocation, unit toggle.
